  - Problem Definition

_During the first phase of the project, your team will collectively brainstorm ideas to identify the potential application area of a robot swarm system and the issues that users/customers face in that area. Otherwise, your team can select one of the default project options provided._

Everyone does this so we have multiple options to choose from.

**Position: IDEA GENERATION (all of us)**

**Due Tuesday by 6pm. Everyone votes by 9pm.**


_Next, as a team, you'll conduct online research to identify (and perhaps talk to) potential customers to better understand their needs. You'll research existing options which may compete with your design. You'll identify how your team's design idea is unique and adds value. You'll start to build a list of references to include in your Final Project Report._

_Based on the results of your research, your team will agree on and write a formal problem definition. This is the problem that your project should seek to resolve._

Position: **RESEARCH**

**Due Wednesday, 6pm.**

_Based on your problem definition, your team will formulate an idea for a specific robot swarm system that can achieve your objective. Each proposed swarm design must contain as many individual robots as there are team members and must contain a list of at least 6 design requirements. Each individual robot within the swarm should have a unique name and function and must be designed to cooperate with the other individual robots within your swarm._

Position: **ROBOT REQUIREMENTS**

**Due Thursday noon.**

_Based on your robot swarm system idea, your team will identify the important tasks and milestones needed to complete your project. As a team, you will create a Gantt Chart to outline the project timeline._

**Due Friday, Noon.**

Position: FACILITATOR AND FINAL DOCUMENT (ME)
